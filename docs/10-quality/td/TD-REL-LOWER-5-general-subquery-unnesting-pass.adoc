== History

At filing, native TPC-H was 18/22 and every remaining decline was subquery-shaped (Q2 multi-table correlated
scalar, Q11 aggregate-HAVING vs scalar subquery, Q18/Q20 IN/EXISTS in expression position), with per-shape arms
that did not compose. The filing proposed Neumann & Kemper's general dependent-join unnesting (BTW 2015).

Scope correction that still applies: the four blockers closed through **bounded composable families**, not a
universal rule seam; the codebase still has no `DependentJoin`/`Apply`/`MarkJoin`. 22/22 is evidence for the
measured corpus, **not** proof of arbitrary subquery unnesting. File a new TD only when a new unsupported family
supplies evidence that the general algebra is cheaper than another bounded rule. Known remaining decline (by
design): correlated scalar `COUNT` declines to legacy for empty-group NULL-vs-0 semantics
(`correlated_scalar_count_declines_count_bug`).

Evidence: row-exact vs DataFusion on the TPC-H ledger; native TPC-H 22/22 (#1068).
