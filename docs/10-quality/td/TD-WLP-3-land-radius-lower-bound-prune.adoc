== History

Ranked blocks by the lower bound `d(q, centroid) − k·radius` with one-pass RMS `block_radii`; `radius_k=0.0` reproduced the exact legacy selection. *Measured (SIFT1M N=100k, 1000 q):* `k=1` did not beat `k=0` at fixed keep (0.8050 vs 0.8087 @0.5) and `k=2` was worse; PR #992's promotion ran under a documented WS8 exception (unpruned 0.9891 vs pruned 0.8087).
*Lesson:* with weak clusters, radii are large and undifferentiated, so a spread-aware bound adds no signal; and a PCA-space radius bound is not a valid correctness guard against a RaBitQ approximate distance (see TD-RDSTRAT-8). Never "fix" a failed prune gate by removing the unpruned differential or allowing a silent full-scan fallback.
