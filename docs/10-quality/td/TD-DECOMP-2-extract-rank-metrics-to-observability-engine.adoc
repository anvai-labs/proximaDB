== History (shipped)

`rank_metrics` (14 tests) moved verbatim into `proximadb-observability-engine`; the root re-exports
it so the 4 callers and the `/metrics/prometheus` scrape of its `OnceLock` statics resolve unchanged.
New edges (engine → rank-core / rank-onnx) are modality → modality.

* Finding: `src/observability` is mostly structurally blocked from a modality-tier crate — the WAL-backed
  `storage/` slice and the axum/tonic `ingestion/adapters/*` would be modality → root/platform edges.
  `rank_metrics` was the one clean, modality-legal leaf; it proved the recipe on a whole module.
