== The finding

The publisher writes deletion vectors only; it never writes data files. Its successor is
therefore the parent's files minus vectors — a complete state only at the parent's capture
LSN plus this delete's tombstones. The completeness precondition (the contract, round 2)
refuses whenever the changed-set since the parent's recorded LSN contains anything the delete
is not publishing, because recording the fence LSN would otherwise hide those changes from
both views.

Consequence, measured by `soak_mixed_workload_holds_the_fence` (tests/iceberg_interop_e2e.rs):

* **Phase A, interleaved inserts and deletes over 40 rounds:** 45 deletes, **0 publications**.
  The first insert after a publication puts a foreign oid in the changed-set, and every later
  delete refuses until the next MATERIALIZE re-records the layout. Both views matched the model
  at every step.
* **Phase B, a chain of deletes right after a MATERIALIZE with no other writes:** every delete
  published (one `dv-live-*` Puffin each) and the OLAP view tracked the model with no
  MATERIALIZE between deletes.

So the fence is correct and works in its eligible shape. That shape (deletes as the only writes
since the last publication) is narrow for an OLTP-plus-analytics table.

The refusal is also *sticky*. A refusal never reaches the layout flip, so the recorded
`snapshot_lsn` stays put, and `changed_oids_since(recorded_lsn)` keeps returning every oid
written after it. The filter removes only the current delete's own oids, so a refused delete's
own tombstone stays in every later delete's changed-set. The trigger does not have to be an
insert: any single refusal (a lost lease, an I/O error, schema drift) makes every later delete
refuse until the next MATERIALIZE re-records the layout, even in the eligible shape.

== Directions (not decided)

. **Ordered-delta reader relaxation** (the contract's "unordered-delta relaxation condition"):
  let `register_merged_olap_table` serve a DV-carrying base together with a NONEMPTY delta when
  the suppress-set provably covers every DV'd oid's tombstone. That is a reader change (the reader
  refuses any nonempty delta against a DV base today, #2256) AND a publisher change: the
  completeness precondition's bail on unexpected oids has to be relaxed, and the publisher has to
  record the parent's LSN instead of the fence LSN. Both halves must be proven again under the
  fence gates.
. **Write the pending inserts too**: publish the changed rows as an appended data file beside
  the vectors (an incremental MATERIALIZE inside the delete). This is broader: it turns the
  delete into a publication of every pending change, with the latency and failure modes that
  implies.
. **Accept the reach** and treat the publisher as a delete-burst optimization, documented as
  such at the activation flip.

== Acceptance

The activation-flip decision records which direction was taken. If the reach is accepted as is
(direction 3), the soak's Phase A pin (zero publications under interleaved inserts, asserted) is the regression baseline. Any relaxation must
keep both views exact under the soak's mixed workload **and** show publications firing there.
