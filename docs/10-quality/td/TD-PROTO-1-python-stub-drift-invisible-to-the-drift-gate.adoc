== History (shipped)

`test_grpc_proto_drift.py` computed only missing stubs/servicers/rpcs, so a message, field or enum change that touches no service
was green by construction (`collection_types.proto` declares no service). The comparison must be **serialized
`FileDescriptorProto`** bytes, not text: of 26 textually diffing files, 12 were pure protoc formatting. The real drift was 5 files
of shape drift (e.g. `PredicateShortfall`, `VectorElementType`, `LookupStage`, fusion `text_query`, all declared on the wire and
unreachable from the SDK, failing as an `AttributeError` that reads as an SDK bug) and 9 files of missing `deprecated` options.

* `test_proto_schema_drift.py` (in `make proto-check`) was **observed failing** on an injected one-field change (old gate 3 passed,
  new gate 2 failed). On the unfixed tree it failed on exactly the 14 measured files. 33 stubs were regenerated.
* Guards on the guard: `test_there_is_something_to_compare` (a parametrised gate whose list silently empties passes forever), and
  `test_the_two_stub_trees_agree`.
* #1706 found an orphaned `proximadb_sdk/v1/proximadb/v1/timeseries_pb2.py` committed under protoc's nested path, whose source proto
  no longer existed. It was deleted, and the gate no longer skips what it cannot see.

== Open: collapse the two stub trees

`clients/python/src/proximadb_sdk/v{1,2}/` and `clients/python/src/proximadb/v{1,2}/` are **both live** —
`unified_client_async.py`, `fusion_v2.py` and `document_v2.py` all import from the `proximadb.v2`
copy — and they are regenerated independently (before the fix, `v1/document_pb2` and `v1/hybrid_pb2` disagreed).
Two independently-generated copies of one contract
is the duplication ADR-029 removed for code chunking, in a different medium. Collapsing is the real
fix; asserting equality is the cheap one that stops the bleeding. The two stub trees were deliberately **not collapsed**
in #1703: that is a wider change — every importer of one package has to
move — and it wants its own review rather than riding in on a gate fix. The equality assertion
will fail loudly if anyone regenerates one tree without the other.
