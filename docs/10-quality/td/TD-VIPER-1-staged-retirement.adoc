== History (shipped)

* *S1 (#1770):* SUPPORTED_SURFACE → Deprecated; factory `warn!` on every VIPER construction; VIPER removed from benchmark promotion sweeps while conformance coverage remains.
* *S2 (`e55df2325`):* collection create/update rejects NEW VIPER selections with a migration pointer (SST default, NOVA columnar, DataFusion/Parquet for warehouse analytics); the context-free factory remains for existing reads.

*Why retire:* #1743 found VIPER's point-read broken for its own writer's format (`FixedSizeList` written vs `ListArray` read) with zero production exposure; `factory.rs` hardcodes `FixedSizeList(Float32, 128)` (#1768); the niche is covered by DataFusion/Parquet (analytics), SST (ANN default) and NOVA (columnar vector). Further VIPER-side unit/type inconsistencies are recorded in TD-DSEFF-2 and are subsumed here.
