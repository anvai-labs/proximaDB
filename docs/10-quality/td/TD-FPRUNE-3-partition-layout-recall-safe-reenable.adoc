= TD-FPRUNE-3: the tag-aware partition layout costs unfiltered recall at scale — default OFF until a recall-safe re-enable is measured

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Field | Value
| Status | **Open.** Layout flipped to default OFF (opt-in `PROXIMADB_PAX_PARTITION_LAYOUT=1`) on 2026-10-29. Re-enabling it needs the gate below.
| Priority | P1 for anyone who declares `filterable_columns`. The shipped default silently lost ~12 points of unfiltered recall@10 at 3M rows.
| Component | `src/storage/engines/sst/block_cluster.rs` (`cluster_plan_ivf_probe_partitioned`, `partition_layout_enabled`); engaged in `src/storage/engines/sst/segment_format.rs` (compaction cluster plan, `shred_spec.first()`).
| Relates to | link:TD-FPRUNE-1-filter-aware-cascade-arc.adoc[TD-FPRUNE-1] (M2/C2 introduced the layout; C1 is its filtered byte win), ADR-089
|===

== Finding

The M2/C2 layout runs k-means separately within each value of the first declared filterable
column, giving `k_g ∝ group share` cells per group in one shared PCA space. With an uncorrelated tag
(`category = i % 10`), every query's true neighbours are spread over all groups, while the global
probe budget (~21 cells/query here) covers only one or two cells per group.

Measured on the settled 3M SIFT128 e1p bed (MinIO + toxiproxy, object_cold, n=1000, same binary
`08f25648a`, same harness args):

[cols="3,2,1,1,1,1", options="header"]
|===
| Arm | Cells | Cells probed/q | recall@10 | MB/q | Region-D MB/q
| filterable columns declared, layout ON (the then default) | 10 + 100 | 21 of 105 | **0.855** | 149.9 | 69.7
| same, residual tail OFF | 10 + 100 | 21 of 105 | 0.865 | 168.4 | 89.4
| no filterable columns | 8 + 50 | 16 of 58 | 0.9706 | 171.4 | 69.5
| filterable columns declared, layout OFF | 96 + 96 | 40 of 192 | **0.9716** | 133.2 | 44.0
|===

The arms do not share a probe budget (the reader's nprobe follows the cell count), so the ON/OFF pair
is not an equal-probe comparison. The conclusion does not rest on it: the no-schema arm reaches 0.9706
at a smaller budget (16 of 58, ~28%) than the ON arm (21 of 105, ~20%), and both layout-ON arms sit
at 0.855–0.865 regardless of the residual tail.

The 100-cell segment is exactly 10 groups × 10 cells. The "unfiltered recall neutral (Δ=0.000)"
evidence the flip-ON relied on was toy-scale (a 512-row unit corpus comparing *filtered* recall,
and a 10k ratchet that declares no filterable columns), so it never exercised this case. The bed
only became measurable once R6 (#2284) let schema-bearing beds settle.

== Re-enable gate

Re-enable the layout (default or per collection) only with a change that keeps unfiltered recall:

* *(a) partition-aware probe budget.* For an unfiltered query on a partitioned segment, scale nprobe
  with the group count (or probe per group), and measure the GET/byte cost of doing so.
* *(b) correlation-gated layout.* Partition only when the tag correlates with the vector clusters,
  measured at compaction (e.g. cell purity of a global k-means against the tag); uncorrelated tags
  keep the global layout.

Gate: on the 3M schema bed, layout ON reaches recall@10 ≥ 0.97 unfiltered AND keeps C1's filtered
byte cut (filtered cat3 bytes/q below the layout-OFF arm), n=1000, object_cold.

== Open questions

* *Filtered cost of the flip is unmeasured at 3M.* Turning the layout off gives up C1's filtered byte
  cut for declared-schema collections; the filtered cat3 arm on the layout-OFF bed is the measurement.
* *Layout-OFF geometry looks odd.* The layout-OFF schema bed settled at 96 + 96 cells, where the
  smaller segment covers only 338,800 rows (~3.5k rows/cell) and reports `coarse_trained_rows`
  2,661,200 — the larger segment's row count — while the no-schema bed settled at 8 + 50. Either a
  harness reporting mix-up or a shared/collection model (TD-PAXDIR-1) being reused across segments;
  check before any re-enable measurement leans on cell counts. The two segments look *swapped*: the
  larger segment (2,661,200 rows) reports `coarse_trained_rows` 49,282, so either the reporter swaps
  the field or the large segment's 96 cells were trained on ~49k rows — which bounds how far the
  0.9716 baseline itself can be trusted (the no-schema bed's large segment also reports a partial
  1,384,800 of 2,769,200).
* *Probed-cell totals are fractional on the layout-ON arms* (`cells_total_per_query` 105.05 / 105.28
  against a settled 10 + 100): about 5 cells per query are not counted, presumably queries where one
  segment takes a non-probe path. Unexplained; it does not change the recall conclusion.
* *Value semantics changed.* The gate used to treat anything except `0/off/false/no` as ON; it now
  accepts only `1/true/on/yes`. Only an operator who enabled it with a non-standard value is affected.
