== History

* **Capability.** One server-side chunk-size policy was declared three times (wire, Rust mirror, SDK),
  none connected. Now only the SDK's `SizingPolicy(window=…, overlap=Fraction(…, Of.WINDOW))` remains —
  the only one that ever chunked anything. If server-side chunking ships, it gets a *new* proto field
  with decided semantics; the boundary-equivalence test (wire policy vs SDK policy produce identical
  boundaries on the ADR-091 corpus) becomes that work's acceptance test.
* **Decisive finding.** A settable field that does nothing is worse than an absent one, and the names
  lied: `size_tokens` counted *characters* at a hardcoded 4× ratio in two of four strategies (the
  "refine with token count" comment had no second pass), and `Heading` silently fell back to paragraph.
  On CJK the 4× ratio can overflow a 512-token model and trigger silent provider truncation.
  `Fraction` names its referent explicitly so the SDK cannot adopt the wire reading by accident.
* **Lessons.** (a) Removal by reservation, not rename, prevents a future field inheriting the lying
  semantics. (b) The Rust deletion broke `proximadb-server` while `cargo check -p proximadb-embedding`
  passed — a per-crate check is not a build. (c) Regenerating Python stubs surfaced pre-existing stub
  drift invisible to the services-only drift gate; filed as TD-PROTO-1.
