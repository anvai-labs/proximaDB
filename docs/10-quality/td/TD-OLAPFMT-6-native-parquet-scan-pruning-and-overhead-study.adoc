= TD-OLAPFMT-6: Native Parquet scan on the pruning ladder + dual-write overhead study

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Field | Value
| Status | Partial — native scan pruning + exact filtered scans landed 2026-10-18 behind `PROXIMADB_NATIVE_FILTERED_SCAN` (opt-in); promotion and SF≥1 study open
| Priority | P1
| Component | `src/query/execution/native_parquet_scan.rs` (no min/max row-group pruning today), `src/network/postgres/relational_pipeline.rs::shadow_probe_native_parquet` skip-guards
| Relates to | ADR-054 (operator cutover, ledger-gated), ADR-052, TD-OLAP-15, TD-OLAPFMT-2
|===

Give the native Parquet scan the engine-neutral pruning ladder (row-group min/max, SBBF,
`parquet_page_prune::page_row_selection`) and lift the "filtered scan" skip-guard so native gets
TPC samples. Measure, at TPC-DS SF≥1: Bloom/page-index bytes, flush CPU, and per-query bytes/latency per
engine; record in BENCHMARK_EVIDENCE.toml.

== Landed (first slice, 2026-10-18)

* `ParquetScanOperator::with_predicates`: footer row-group skip
  (`parquet_page_prune::row_group_prunable`, new) + page-index `RowSelection`
  (`page_row_selection`) — the same functions and semantics as the DataFusion provider;
  recorded into IoTrace (`splits_*`, `pages_*`, `page_rows_skipped`).
* Native lowering applies `Scan.predicate` exactly as a `FilterProject` above the injected
  parquet source (pruning is a superset); expressions native cannot lower decline to DF.
* `try_native_over_parquet` serves filtered single-table scans when
  `PROXIMADB_NATIVE_FILTERED_SCAN=1` (default off until ledger parity, ADR-054 promotion).
