Both files are self-contained over external crates only. The crate later also received `timeout`
(link:TD-DECOMP-48-extract-timeout-mw.adoc[TD-DECOMP-48]) and `backpressure` (link:TD-DECOMP-49-extract-backpressure-mw.adoc[TD-DECOMP-49]).

* Lesson (recurring across the program — this is its canonical statement): a `use`-statement dep scan
  misses fully-qualified, test-only and derive-macro deps. Here `serde_json` (tests), `thiserror`
  (`#[derive(Error)]`, whose absence cascaded to a missing-`Display` E0599) and `toml` were found only by
  compiling. Compile the standalone crate early, before the full-tree check.
