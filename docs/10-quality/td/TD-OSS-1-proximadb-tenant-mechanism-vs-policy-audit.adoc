== History

The rule: **mechanism = OSS, policy = commercial.** `TenantContext`, `UnifiedUserContext`/`RbacTenantContext`, `UnifiedPermission`
(40 neutral capability variants), `tenant_tier_types` (`Tier1`..`Tier5`; commercial names survive only as serde aliases) and the
inert-default ports (`LimitsResolver`, `set_tier_multiplier_resolver`, `TierPolicy` parser) stay OSS. Tier-to-multiplier values,
paid-tier quota caps, enterprise RBAC policy and billing compute go to the commercial crate, which *depends on* these OSS types and
does not own them. Verdict: #901 (RBAC identity into the tenant crate) is boundary-correct, and no type moves.

Re-run check (expect empty):

[source,bash]
----
grep -rniE "enterprise|business|premium|dedicated|paid|pro|team|starter|free|trial|sku|commercial|sale|license" \
  crates/foundation/proximadb-tenant/src/ | grep -vE "^\s*//|serde|alias|test|doc"
