== History

The ABAC policy key was stamped per-surface by optional resolvers (REST middleware,
`GrpcAuthContext`, Flight inline `stable_id_of`) — N resolution points, N drift seams — and crates
below the network layer could not see the identity at all (the REST Extension was the root-crate
`MiddlewareTenantContext`). Making the **foundation** type the carrier (visible to every crate) is
what turned TD-ABAC-9 (api-crate consumers) and TD-ABAC-10 (pgwire) into mechanical constructors.
`MiddlewareTenantContext` keeps surface-local extras and derives its identity core from it.
