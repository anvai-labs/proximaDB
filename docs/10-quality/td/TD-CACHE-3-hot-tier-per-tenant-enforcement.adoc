== History (shipped)

* **S1 (ambient route):** instead of threading tenant through six search signatures, the tenant is
  read from the per-request `io_trace::instrument` task-local (`io_trace::current_tenant()`), so cache
  keys are real-tenant; per-tenant hit/miss/bytes export as bounded series (top-50 by bytes);
  admission floors/weights come from the same `PROXIMADB_CACHE_TIERS_PATH` `TierPolicy` the footer
  cache uses. Caveat that still applies: task-locals do not cross un-propagated `tokio::spawn` —
  the identical constraint all io_trace metering has.
* **S2 true-pin:** `CacheBudget::with_pin_reserve(frac ≤ 0.5)` carves a reserve OUT of the shared
  moka pool; a tenant with a tier floor pins into its floor segment (invisible to TinyLFU ⇒ never
  evictable by other tenants), with within-tenant LRU recycle and graceful degradation to
  admission-only when floors oversubscribe the reserve (shared + reserve = total, no overcommit).
  Default-OFF (`PROXIMADB_SURVIVOR_PIN_RESERVE_FRAC`). Lesson: an admission-only floor is not a
  pin — TinyLFU eviction is floor-agnostic.
* **S3 metering:** `proximadb_survivor_cache_tenant_{pinned,entitled}_bytes`; sustained
  pinned < entitled under pressure is the capacity signal (B4ms → E-series).

Evidence: two-tenant Azurite bed (`scripts/bench/two_tenant_bed.py`, 2026-07-26) — tenant A pinned
13.24 MB = 98.7% of entitlement, byte-identical through tenant B's 9,162-admission churn.

== Open
