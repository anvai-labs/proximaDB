== History (shipped)

Found in the 2026-07-23 restart/recovery pressure tests (#1172). The multiplexer forwarded HTTP/2 to a compile-time
`127.0.0.1:15679`. A second instance's internal bind failed with a log-only error, startup still reported success, and its
gRPC/Flight traffic was served by the first instance. Proof: `flush_collection` on B appeared in A's log 14 times, and `lsof`
showed A's pid. Lesson: an ignored bind failure turns a port collision into silent cross-instance (and cross-tenant) routing.
S1 derives the upstream per instance; S2 makes the bind failure fatal (fail-closed).
