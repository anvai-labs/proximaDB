== History

On qa-promotion #992 the WS8 job failed only on the VOE centroid-prune leg: cascade recall@10 0.9891 (pass) vs pruned 0.8087 < 0.90 floor (15,000 of 35,000 block visits pruned). The gate was intentionally fail-closed and advisory (develop→qa requires only `CI Success`), blocking only the prune's default-ON flip.
*Pattern that still applies:* a recall gate for a recall-affecting optimization must assert the optimization actually engages and compare against a same-run unoptimized baseline — a gate that passes because nothing was pruned proves nothing (reused by TD-RDSTRAT-8).
