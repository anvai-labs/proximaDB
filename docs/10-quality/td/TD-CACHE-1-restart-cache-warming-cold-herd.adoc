== History (shipped)

Cold-vs-hot is a ~150× QPS and ~500× $/QPS gap; a restart converts the entire working set to cold
at once (the Spot-eviction herd). Shipped design (co-design revision during implementation): **no
blanket boot sweep** — warming is *demand-proven* (per-tenant `warm_cache.json` manifests: top-K
survivor ranges by hit count, emitted best-effort on SIGTERM / eviction notice under the tenant
`_cache/` prefix) or *contract-driven*; idle/free tenants stay lazy first-touch, so Spot churn costs
zero speculative GETs. Boot replay runs per tenant inside `io_trace::instrument` (tenant-correct keys,
replay GETs metered to the tenant), replays fresh (≤24 h) entries via coalesced read-through, and
prefills CONTROL invariants (header/footer/A0) through a purpose-built helper (the search path's
reads are rank-interleaved and were not reusable). Advisory and mixed-safe: absent/corrupt/stale
manifest ⇒ cold start; compacted-away paths skipped. Kill-switch `PROXIMADB_CACHE_PREFILL=0`.
S3 orders replay by tier (`warming::tier_ordered`, `TierSpec.weight` desc) — tier decides *when*,
manifest presence decides *whether*. S4 OSS half: opt-in `PROXIMADB_EVICTION_WATCH=azure|aws|gcp`
IMDS poll emits manifests and flushes the memtable on the first preemption notice.

Evidence at filing: hot path 1,350–2,786 QPS, 0 GETs (#1172 T3); cold 3.9 QPS c=1 / 18.2 QPS ceiling.

== Non-goals
Whole-corpus RAM residency (the Qdrant model — surrendered cost advantage);
warming cold tenants nobody queries; any read-path format change.
