== Everything is opt-in today

* **Residency** requires three operator choices, all absent by default:
  1. a pool declared in `storage.reservation_pools` (id, local-disk path,
     capacity) — an empty list leaves the registry closed and every
     PERFORMANCE admission refused with an actionable error;
  2. `default_grant_bytes` on the default pool (0 ⇒ refuse);
  3. a collection created (or updated) with `storage_class: "performance"`
     on an object-store-backed SST placement (SC2 admission).
  Kill switch: `PROXIMADB_TIER1_RESERVATIONS_OFF=1` refuses admission
  without erasing the ledger (fail-closed on a non-UTF-8 value).
* **Access tier** is per-collection `default_access_tier` (hot/cool/cold),
  applied to future compaction output only; omitted ⇒ the account default.
  L0 flush output and staging keep the account default (minimum-duration
  economics — see the worksheet).
* **Balanced** collections are byte-for-byte today's behavior: no pool, no
  admission, no tier, no status change beyond the `active` projection.

== Operator drills (each names its invariant)

* **Disk loss:** revoke a pool's disk → serving continues remotely at recall
  parity, and the status depends on the failure shape: the store re-opening
  EMPTY reports WARMING (coverage 0-of-N within capacity — never read
  WARMING as a gate failure); the store absent or failing to open reports
  DEGRADED; a surviving inventory over budget reports DEGRADED. Re-provision
  → the backfill restores READY (SC-TE7).
* **Capacity pressure:** fill the disk with an over-admitted grant → pins
  hold until their grant is released; oversize pins are refused at admission
  (SC-TE4/6).
* **Restart:** reopen with live grants → budget re-armed at open, groups
  re-registered, backfill warms live segments, READY returns under the same
  g once coverage completes.
* **Idle accounting:** an untouched granted collection accrues reservation
  intervals hourly (coalesced writes), independent of query traffic (SC-TE9).

== What NOT to do

* Do not point a pool at a RAM-backed path (tmpfs) — the reservation
  contract assumes real local disk.
* Do not set `default_access_tier` on a local placement (refused: the tier
  would be silently dropped) or `archive` (refused: archived objects cannot
  serve).
* Do not quote prices from the worksheet before re-rating the dated
  competitor worksheet per the design's normalization rules.
* Do not enable `PROXIMADB_TYPED_PATHS` mid-soak for collections whose
  grants were admitted under the legacy layout without re-checking grant
  prefixes (both layouts are registered, but a flip changes where flush
  writes).
