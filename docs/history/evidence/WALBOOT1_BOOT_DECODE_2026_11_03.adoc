= TD-WALBOOT-1 boot-decode measurement — linear, bytes-bound, worst when recovering

Harness: `wal_boot_summary_rebuild_cost_measurement` (`#[ignore]`, advisory) in
`src/services/canonical_wal.rs` — seals N segments (rotation at 1 byte, one append per
segment, ~500 B records), then times `FramedTableWalAppender::open()` three times per N.
Run: `cargo test --lib wal_boot_summary_rebuild_cost -- --ignored --nocapture`, **dev
profile** (release numbers are the follow-up gate; the linear SHAPE is this artifact's
finding). Host: aiserver1 (WSL2, 16 vCPU, 125 GB), /tmp on ext4, light load.

== Data (2026-11-03)

| Sealed segments | Bytes | open ms (1st / 2nd / 3rd) |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | 3,928 | 2 / 1 / 1 |
| 32 | 15,734 | 5 / 5 / 5 |
| 128 | 62,995 | 22 / 20 / 20 |
| 512 | 252,948 | 93 / 81 / 80 |

Fit: linear, **~0.32 ms per KB** (dev), stable across cold→warm opens (the decode is the
cost, not the disk — page-cache-warm opens improve ≈10–15%).

== Reading

1. **The cost is bytes, not segment count** — `read_sealed_segment` decodes whole files, so
   the summary rebuild is O(retained WAL bytes). Segment granularity is irrelevant to the
   cost; a sidecar summary per segment saves the decode at any granularity.
2. **The worst case lands at the worst time.** A healthy WAL truncates below
   `min_unflushed_lsn` and stays small; the retained WAL grows to hundreds of MB exactly
   when truncation is pinned — a poisoned store (TD-WALPOISON-1), ambiguous oids
   (TD-USUB-19), or object-store outage — and the recovery for every one of those states is
   **restart**. Extrapolated (dev): 0.5 GB ≈ 2.6 min; even a 10× release-profile
   improvement puts a 2 GB pin-recovery boot at ~a minute. The sidecar-summary candidate is
   justified by this data; the off-lock-truncation candidate still needs its
   append-latency measurement (the truncate/rotate hold histograms shipped with this
   instrumentation capture it in operation — no dedicated harness yet).

== Limitations

Dev-profile absolutes (release re-run pending); tiny uniform records (~500 B) rather than
mixed payloads; single host, light load; extrapolation beyond 253 KB is linear-fit, not
measured. None of these plausibly change the SHAPE; they bound the magnitude only.
