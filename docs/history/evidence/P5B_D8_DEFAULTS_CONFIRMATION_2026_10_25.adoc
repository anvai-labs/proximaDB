== Bed realignment (owner decision, 2026-10-25)

No cross-region deployments are planned, so the **canonical gate profile is intra-region**: Toxiproxy 5 + 5 ms (measured 11.4 ms RTT through the proxy), matching intra-region S3 GET p50 (~5–15 ms) with headroom. The prior 50 ms profile (D7's gate run, and this run's stress arms) is retained as a **robustness column**, not the gate. Waves and requests are latency-independent; p50 re-prices accordingly.

Same bed otherwise: MinIO single drive behind the proxy, SIFT1M (1,000,000 × 128, L2), full-corpus ground truth, top-10, 1,000 queries/phase (disjoint ranges), concurrency 1, `--read-ranges-inflight 16`, `--training-compaction-min-mb 1 --max-segments 3`, planner pinned to 4 MiB coalesce range, `systemd-run -p MemoryMax=64G`, arms serialized on an idle box.

Default cell geometry (k_G): the settled beds carry ~34 cells per 1M-row segment (old rule: ~15); each query probes 11–12 cells (~34–41% of rows).

== Known costs and follow-ups (from the D8 review)

* `k_G` sizes against the default RaBitQ + SQ8 geometry; a collection with a non-default rerank quant sizes against that assumption (follow-up: plumb the actual per-tier bytes).
* Pre-D8-trained collections retrain their coarse model once on the next compaction (old k ≈ 15 vs needed ≈ 34 > 2× growth trigger) — a one-time cost, mixed-read-safe.
* The D5 warm-planning interaction documented in D7's artifact (one merged B range re-fetched per repeat) remains and is inside the warm budget.
