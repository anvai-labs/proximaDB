== Bed

Same as the D8 canonical profile: MinIO single drive behind Toxiproxy 5 + 5 ms (measured ~13 ms RTT through the proxy), SIFT1M 1M × 128, full-corpus ground truth, top-10, 1,000 queries per phase (disjoint per phase, identical across arms), concurrency 1, `--read-ranges-inflight 16`, `--training-compaction-min-mb 1 --max-segments 3`, `--centroid-directory default` — the PAXDIR *feature* gates unset, i.e. server defaults; the sweep's own gates (recall ≥ 0.98, requests ≤ 20, cold waves ≤ 3, warm ≤ 2) were ARMED on every arm, and the 4 MiB arm failed the requests gate), memory-capped (`MemoryMax=64G`), arms sequential on an idle box. The intended tuning variable: `--coalesce-range-mb` 4 / 16 / 40 (the SC0-exposed flag; gap fixed at the historical 1 MiB). Fresh-bed training also changed geometry (see below), so this is not a single-variable experiment. Every arm's result.json records its cap (the `coalescing` and `range_planner` blocks agree — the SC0 review fix).

== Findings (interpretation corrected by reconciliation, 2026-10-26)

. The 16 MiB arm measured 47.4% fewer cold requests than the 4 MiB arm, with similar
  recall and the same median wave depth. These are observed configurations, not an
  isolated causal estimate of changing only the cap. [M]
. The 40 MiB arm measured 2.2% fewer requests than 16 MiB. A plateau is a hypothesis
  worth testing, not an established knee from one run per arm. [M/D]
. Geometry was not fixed: settled cells 21/25/26, and the 16 MiB arm probed 10.8% fewer
  rows than the 4 MiB arm. The interaction with extents, coalescing gaps and request count
  is nonlinear. Comparing percent changes in rows and requests does not bound this
  confound or prove the headline causal effect. Replay identical objects/model/ETags. [M/U]
. The 4 MiB arm failed the <=20 request gate: 20.005, not a rounded pass. Recall held in
  all arms; cold waves p50=3 and p95=4. Preserve every gate result. [M]
. Median latency increased from 107 to 129 ms at 16 MiB; p95 from 149 to 181 ms;
  QPS decreased from 9.22 to 7.61. Without matched geometry/repeats, neither a causal
  regression nor latency neutrality is established. The unchanged wave count is not
  proof of unchanged latency: bytes, queueing and computation also contribute. [M/U]

== Cost read [D — derived, S3 us-east-1 list rates on the measured units]

Cold request cost at 1M scale, both sides from THIS sweep's units: 4 MiB ≈ $12.60/M (19.005 GET × $0.40/M + 1 LIST × $5.00/M) → 16 MiB ≈ $8.81/M (9.514 × $0.40 + $5.00). (The D8-era $12.42/M figure came from a different bed's 18.55 GETs; do not mix the two.) For the measured 16 MiB configuration, **the discovery LIST is 57% of the cold request bill** — TD-PAXDIR-2 (listing cache) is the dominant remaining request lever, ahead of any further cap change.

== Production posture (no default change made)

The harness's historical 4 MiB pin understates production: `IopsBudget::S3` targets 8 MiB per range (16 MiB max) for S3/GCS paths, 4 MiB for Azure, 1 MiB local — these configured limits do not establish that production latency or request counts interpolate between the pilot arms. No production default changes in this PR. Per the design's stop rule, a default change would first require the finalist caps on fragmented segments, filtered queries, 10M rows and 768d before any global flip.
