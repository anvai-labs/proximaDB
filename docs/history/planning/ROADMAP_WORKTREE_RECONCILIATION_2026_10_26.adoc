== Direction corrections and session nudges

These are durable pickup instructions. There is no verified live-session messaging channel
in this environment; recording them here does not claim they were delivered to a running agent.

=== Track B — keep v3-first and close the read/write contract

* The observed WIP is aligned with readers-before-writer: Parquet/DataFusion and DuckDB
  reads apply DVs, successor append carries DVs, and the new interop test publishes DVs
  test-side. None of that is merged or a live incremental-delete publisher yet.
* Verify file-position masks before filter/limit, offset accounting after row-group pruning,
  hidden columns, count/statistics, cache invalidation, old snapshots and append races.
  Keep carry-through tests: publishing inherited data files without their DVs resurrects rows.
* ADR-097 now records the owner's v3-first direction. `iceberg_needs_v3` moved to
  `proximadb-catalog-schema::object_store_bridge`; the never-downgrade floor landed in
  #2245, but default v3/upgrade activation remains unimplemented. Do not add a new v2
  delete writer; prove v3 row lineage and external update/delete read-back before activation.
* TD-SEAM-1's tenant-scoped REST metadata-key blocker and production facade reachability
  still gate the bulk-append lane. Engine tests do not close this caller-level gate.

=== Track E — SC1 scaffold is not complete SC2 admission

* The WIP's NOT_ACTIVE status for economy/performance is aligned with the roadmap.
  Its TD text says "landed" and generated SDKs complete before a PR/merge exists;
  change those claims to WIP and attach the actual validation evidence at publication.
* `check_performance_admission` currently checks SST and a recognized URL scheme using
  the request's `storage_path`. That is neither an authorization check against configured
  operator targets nor a check of the final resolved assignment. Cover allowed configured
  defaults, rejected arbitrary tenant prefixes, restart and immutable-placement updates.
  Call this partial SC2 until those tests pass; don't move location authorization to SC3.
* `storage_class_status` computes generation as 0/1 from field presence while the WIP
  already allows policy updates. Persist and increment the generation on accepted changes,
  or explicitly defer updates; don't expose a monotonic-generation contract with constant 1.
* At the audit point, generated OpenAPI/SDK diffs were absent from the dirty-file list.
  Verify each facade's desired policy AND inactive status, especially the v2 read model
  that currently echoes only a class string. An inactive performance policy must not look active.
* Retain one config owner and compatibility tests. SC3 quota/reservation and SC4 protected
  coverage remain separate; schema/API churn is not permission to claim resident capacity.

=== Experiment lane — keep the pilot, repair the causal claim

#2249 measured 20.005 / 10.514 / 10.282 cold requests per query for caps 4/16/40 MiB.
All recalls exceed 0.98; p50 waves are 3 and p95 waves 4. The arms used 21/25/26 cells,
fresh beds, and one trial each. Therefore they are measurements at three configurations,
not a fixed-object causal estimate. A percent change in rows cannot bound a nonlinear
coalescing/request-count confound. Median latency increased 107 → 129 ms at 16 MiB;
without repeats it is neither proven regression nor proven neutrality.

Next: reuse identical objects/model/ETags and query IDs, change only cap, repeat arms and
record missing CPU/RSS/p99/in-flight bytes/useful-vs-overread/request sizes. Preserve the
4 MiB request-gate failure; don't round it into a pass. No default flip. TD-PAXDIR-2 remains
an independent next lever, subject to coherent discovery rather than a TTL-only shortcut.
