`BIGANN_NPROBE_GEOMETRY_100K_10M_2026_08_04.adoc`. It does not authorize a

production configuration recommendation.

== Result

[cols="1,1,1,1,1", options="header"]
|===
| Rows | `k_c` | top-10 economy / quality | top-20 economy / quality | max/mean cell rows

| 100K | 3 | 2 / 3 | 2 / 3 | 1.17
| 330K | 10 | 3 / 5 | 3 / 5 | 1.66
| 1M | 30 | 5 / 6 | 6 / 7 | 1.48
| 3.3M | 100 | 8 / 16 | 8 / 16 | 1.73
| 10M | 305 | 22 / 22 | 26 / 26 | 2.63
|===

The sampled floor-minus-one points are negative controls, not inferred
thresholds: top-10 recall moved 0.9778→0.9887 at `k_c=3` (nprobe 2→3),
0.9767→0.9838 at `k_c=10` (4→5), and 0.9678→0.9809 at `k_c=30`
(5→6). Top-20 moved 0.9771→0.9898, 0.97355→0.9823, and
0.9777→0.98495 at the same three anchors. The `k_c=100` and `k_c=305`
matrices did not sample exact floor-minus-one points; no such claim is made.

"Economy" is the natural physical-GET knee. "Quality" is the first measured
point with recall at or above 0.98. They are different objectives even where
the same point happens to satisfy both. At 10M the quality points measured:

* top-10, nprobe 22: recall 0.9804, 52.902 physical GET/query,
  130.86 MB/query, 416.76 ms p50;
* top-20, nprobe 26: recall 0.98285, 64.340 physical GET/query,
  163.39 MB/query, 511.19 ms p50.

The fitted quality curves are descriptive within `k_c=3..305`:

* top-10: `1.7202765 * k_c^0.4466996` (log R-squared 0.9608,
  leave-one-scale-out MAPE 16.89%);
* top-20: `1.6639694 * k_c^0.4751678` (log R-squared 0.9858,
  leave-one-scale-out MAPE 11.86%).

The fit is not itself a recall proof: it under-predicts the measured
`k_c=100` floor. More importantly, interpolation between these anchors is not
portable across corpora. At `k_c=90`, both BIGANN anchor interpolations produce
nprobe 15, while repository BGE measurements at 384 and 768 dimensions require
nprobe 25 for top-10 and approximately 30 for top-20; nprobe 20 was still below
recall 0.98. The production segment path is process-global and has no
collection, corpus-family, dimension, projection-width, seed, metric, or
calibration identity with which to distinguish those workloads.

The proposed `measured_quality_v1` configuration surface is therefore
**REFUTED and removed**. Default-OFF would not have made it correct for a
mixed-collection process. The benchmark binary also predates the rejected PR
head by 433 commits, and selector-unit tests did not exercise
Config→global-settings→real-PAX recall. A future policy must be
collection-scoped, fail closed on calibration-identity mismatch, and pass a
current-head production-route recall matrix covering anchors, interpolation
boundaries, top-k buckets, metrics, dimensions, and representative corpus
families. The shipped geometric behavior remains unchanged; this artifact does
not assert that it is cross-corpus optimal or recall-safe.

== Co-design interpretation

Cell-count tuning alone does not meet the object-storage operation target:
even the 10M quality point costs 52.9 GET/query. Region-A and Region-B fetches,
survivor locality, segment count, and RTT waves remain multiplicative terms.
The cell imbalance rising to 2.63 max/mean makes row/byte-aware stopping a
promising successor, but this matrix does not measure such a policy and does
not authorize one.

Azurite supports relative operation/byte conclusions, not Azure WAN latency
SLAs. A later default flip still requires current-binary, provider-realistic
paired evidence and the repository recall ratchet.
