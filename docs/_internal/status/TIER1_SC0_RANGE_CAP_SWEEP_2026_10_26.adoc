= TD-TIER-1 SC0 / E1: fixed-geometry range-cap sweep (4 / 16 / 40 MiB)

**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Branch:** `bench/tier1-cap-sweep` @ `41ac7d439` (empty anchor over develop `d929d7993`; binary built in-worktree at that HEAD) · **TD:** link:../../10-quality/td/TD-TIER-1-collection-storage-policy-and-residency.adoc[TD-TIER-1] SC0 / design §5–§7 (E1).

== Bed

Same as the D8 canonical profile: MinIO single drive behind Toxiproxy 5 + 5 ms (measured ~13 ms RTT through the proxy), SIFT1M 1M × 128, full-corpus ground truth, top-10, 1,000 queries per phase (disjoint per phase, identical across arms), concurrency 1, `--read-ranges-inflight 16`, `--training-compaction-min-mb 1 --max-segments 3`, `--centroid-directory default` — the PAXDIR *feature* gates unset, i.e. server defaults; the sweep's own gates (recall ≥ 0.98, requests ≤ 20, cold waves ≤ 3, warm ≤ 2) were ARMED on every arm, and the 4 MiB arm failed the requests gate), memory-capped (`MemoryMax=64G`), arms sequential on an idle box. The only inter-arm variable: `--coalesce-range-mb` 4 / 16 / 40 (the SC0-exposed flag; gap fixed at the historical 1 MiB). Every arm's result.json records its cap (the `coalescing` and `range_planner` blocks agree — the SC0 review fix).

== Findings

. **4 → 16 MiB halves requests at unchanged recall and wave depth** (20.00 → 10.51 req/q; recall +0.0007; waves p50 3 in all arms). The added transfer time does not add a wave. Warm requests drop too (6.01 → 4.58). This confirms the pre-sweep model: the D8 trace showed ~10.5 of 18.55 GETs were full 4 MiB Region-B ranges that larger caps merge.
. **16 → 40 MiB is the plateau** (10.514 → 10.282, one arm each, no repeats): the remaining GETs are extents a 16 MiB cap already merges or that widening cannot. The disjoint-useful-bytes mechanism is inferred from the D8 trace's extent layout, not measured per-extent here [D]; the knee the design predicted sits at or before 16 MiB at this geometry [M].
. **Geometry was approximately, not exactly, fixed — and the bias runs BOTH ways**: settled coarse cells 21 / 25 / 26 across arms (training samples the compaction input rows, which vary per fresh bed). Cells *probed* were pinned at ~10 by nprobe in the 4 and 16 arms (9.99 each; 10.99 at 40), so the settled-cell rise added no probe requests; but the 16 arm's smaller cells probed 10.8% fewer rows (422,805 vs 474,122) and 3.9% fewer Region-B bytes — a channel that plausibly REDUCES the 16 arm's requests, i.e. biases modestly *in favor* of the halving. The −50% request effect dwarfs that ±11% channel, and the 4 MiB arm's average 3.1 MB/GET shows extent count (not rows) was the binding term, so the headline stands; recorded without the earlier one-sided "conservative bound" claim. [M]
. **The 4 MiB arm tripped the ≤ 20 requests gate by 0.005** (20.005 = 20,005 requests / 1,000 queries, displayed 20.00): bed-shape variance at the gate boundary, recall held. The sweep's binding constraint is recall (≥ 0.98, held in all arms per the design); the gate trip is recorded, not excused. [M]
. **Latency is a wash within bed variance** (p50 107–129 ms band across arms; per-arm beds differ in cells and rounds). No arm regressed waves, which is the latency term the profile controls. [M]

== Cost read [D — derived, S3 us-east-1 list rates on the measured units]

Cold request cost at 1M scale, both sides from THIS sweep's units: 4 MiB ≈ $12.60/M (19.005 GET × $0.40/M + 1 LIST × $5.00/M) → 16 MiB ≈ $8.81/M (9.514 × $0.40 + $5.00). (The D8-era $12.42/M figure came from a different bed's 18.55 GETs; do not mix the two.) After the halving, **the discovery LIST is 57% of the cold request bill** — TD-PAXDIR-2 (listing cache) is the dominant remaining request lever, ahead of any further cap change.

== Production posture (no default change made)

The harness's historical 4 MiB pin understates production: `IopsBudget::S3` targets 8 MiB per range (16 MiB max) for S3/GCS paths, 4 MiB for Azure, 1 MiB local — so production S3 deployments already sit between the 4 and 16 arms without any env var. No production default changes in this PR. Per the design's stop rule, a default change would first require the finalist caps on fragmented segments, filtered queries, 10M rows and 768d before any global flip.
