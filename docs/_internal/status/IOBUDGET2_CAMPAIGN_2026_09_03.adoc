**Verdict: PASS under the product-approved floor.** Measured against the
previous 0.98 convention this operating point fails (paired −0.0041 ≫ −0.0005;
absolute 0.9794 < 0.98) — and on that evidence the product owner accepted the

(on record): default nprobe = ceil(2·√k_c) probes 11/30 ≈ 37% of cells at k=30
but 23/122 ≈ 19% at k=122 — the probe fraction halves on finer partitions.
This also makes the 10M curve's best measured recall 0.9782 (recorded in
BENCHMARK_EVIDENCE as "0.98 explicitly unattained") attainable. Historical TD
numbers keep their original contexts.

on-default operating point the flip targets.

recall, passes every gate the fixed-policy baseline fails, and does not degrade
