= Roadmap and Ledgers
:toc: left

[.lead]
The roadmap, plus the machine-checked ledgers and baselines CI reads. Support tier is governed by
link:../../SUPPORTED_SURFACE.adoc[SUPPORTED_SURFACE.adoc], not by anything here.

|===
| File | Purpose | Enforced by

| link:STRATEGIC_ROADMAP.adoc[STRATEGIC_ROADMAP.adoc] | Phases, sequencing and the current quarter. Where it disagrees with a TD's own status row, the TD wins. | —
| `BENCHMARK_EVIDENCE.toml` | Every performance claim in narrative docs, with its measured evidence. | `scripts/check_perf_claims.py`
| `CAPABILITY_MATRIX.toml` | Capability-by-surface maturity matrix. | `scripts/validate_capability_matrix.py`
| `PANIC_POLICY_BASELINE.json` | Allowed panic sites, which may only shrink. | `scripts/count_panic_patterns.sh`
| `PANIC_POLICY_WORKSPACE_BASELINE.json` | Allowed panic sites, which may only shrink. | `scripts/count_panic_patterns.sh --scope workspace`
| `SILENT_FAILURE_BASELINE.json` | Silent-failure sites, which may only shrink. | `scripts/count_silent_failures.py`
| `V1_PROTO_USAGE_BASELINE.json` | Remaining v1-proto usage (TD-PROTO-2). | `scripts/check_v1_proto_usage.py`
| `COVERAGE_BASELINE.json` | Coverage floor. | `scripts/coverage_ratchet.py`
|===
