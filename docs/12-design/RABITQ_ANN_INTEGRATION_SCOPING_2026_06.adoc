== History

*Finding that framed P3:* wiring RaBitQ into ANN search was **not** a search-executor tweak. The
kernel, the on-disk codec and the rank→f32-rerank recall contract were already done. The blocker was
that the SST/HELIX/NOVA segments were persisted as row-based `ProximaDataBlock` with raw f32, so the
cold search path never opened a `PaxBlockReader` and RaBitQ codes were never on the read/write path.

*Recall contract it established:* `rabitq_block_scoring_preserves_recall_at_k_with_rerank` in
`proximadb-block-format/src/reader.rs` requires recall@10 ≥ 0.80 at ≥10× compression, by ranking on
codes, reranking the top-K in f32, then taking the top-k. The migration plan's cold-recall gate
superseded it at N=1000 with recall@10 ≥ 0.90.
