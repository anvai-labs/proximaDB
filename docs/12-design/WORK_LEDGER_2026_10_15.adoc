== 1. How the corpus is organised now

* **DONE / SUPERSEDED documents** have been compacted in place to a short `== History` record: what shipped, the decisive finding or lesson, and the PRs. Every file and ID was kept: about 254 TD ids are cited from code, and git history holds the full original text.
* **PARTIAL documents** carry a `== History (shipped)` section for the done slices. Every open slice, gate and nuance is kept verbatim.
* **OPEN / PROPOSED documents** are unchanged in substance. Only literal duplication was replaced by cross-links.
* Several status lines were wrong when checked against git and code: about 100 across the corpus. All were corrected with evidence.
* A fresh-context verification pass re-checked every compacted Done/Superseded document (104 files) against the original text. It found and restored, verbatim, 4 open items that compaction had dropped: TD-DELVEC-1, TD-FLIGHT-1 (back to Partial), TD-UICDN-1 and TD-REL-LOWER-1.

Local CLAUDE/GEMINI/AGENTS.md copies should be re-synced from the seeds.
* **ADRs whose status lags reality.**
** ADR-094 is still *Proposed*, although its amendments are applied (ADR-027 reads "accepted as narrowed by ADR-094") and Phases 1–4 have merged. It needs acceptance.
** ADR-023, 030, 055, 089 and 091 are implemented but were never accepted; 021 is gated on P9.
** ADR-036 is *Proposed* while mandate #6 treats it as binding.
* **ID collision.** ADR-039 cites TD-181 for DataFusion leakage guards, but TD-181 is the catalog OID migration. No leakage grep ratchet exists.
* **Decisions not executed or never filed.**
** ADR-006 §3 decided to archive SWIFT/RAPTOR; they are only feature-gated.
** ADR-053 names TD-XMODAL-1/2/3/5 and ADR-035 names TD-SC-1..5; none were ever filed.
* **Roadmap contradictions, flagged in STRATEGIC_ROADMAP.**
** Raft (2027 vs "done Mar 2026" vs open), DiskANN (2027 vs Q4 2026) and serverless (2028 vs Phase 8 in progress) disagree across documents.
** The MVP list contradicts the roadmap's own phase tables.
* **Code comments that drift from code.**
** `sst/flush/mod.rs` documents two defaults as "off" that are now on (PAX row-group layout, compaction arming).
